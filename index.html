What is Orbit?+

Orbit is a product concept for a calmer team workspace, bringing tasks, projects, and conversations together. This page includes an interactive preview so you can explore the experience.

Can I try Orbit for free?+

Yes. Choose “Start for free” to personalize the sample workspace in your browser. This demo doesn’t require a credit card or create an online account.

Does it work with the tools I already use?+

The concept includes connections with Slack, Figma, Notion, GitHub, Google Drive, and Linear. Integration cards on this page describe the planned workflows; they don’t connect to your accounts.

Is Orbit a good fit for a small team?+

Orbit is designed around small product teams and growing creative teams. The Free concept plan gives a team of up to three people a simple starting point.

Can I change my plan later?+

The proposed plans are designed to grow with your team. Pricing on this concept page is illustrative, and no payments or subscriptions are processed.
